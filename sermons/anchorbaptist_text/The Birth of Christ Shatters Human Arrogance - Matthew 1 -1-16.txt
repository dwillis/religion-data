I pray that today would be the day that they understand that they are a sinner, that their sin separates them from a holy God, but that Christ paid in full the penalty for their sins on the cross. He rose from the dead, and by trusting in what he has done for them, they can receive free forgiveness of sins by grace alone, through faith alone in Christ. We ask it in Christ's name, Amen.
