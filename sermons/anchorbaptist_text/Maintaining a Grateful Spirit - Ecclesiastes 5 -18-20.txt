Father, help us to enjoy the gifts that you've given us. Whether it's our family, whether it's the job that we do, whether it's the position that you have set us in. Sin in this society, in this community. Father, whatever it is that we have begun to be ungrateful for it, I pray that you would convict us of that sin. And that you would draw us to a heart of gratitude and humility. I pray that we would be a thriving and contented people. We ask these things in Christ's name. Amen.
